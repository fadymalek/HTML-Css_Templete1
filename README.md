## Circuit Quest — electricity missions (grades 4–5)

`circuit-quest/index.html` is a self-contained interactive game (one HTML file, no install) with 8 missions that get harder step by step:

| # | Mission | Level | What kids practise |
|---|---------|-------|--------------------|
| 1 | Close the Loop | Easy | Spot circuits that make a full loop from + to − |
| 2 | Conductor Detective | Easy | Sort objects into conductors and insulators |
| 3 | Wire Repair Shop | Medium | Fix gaps and bare wires with copper and tape |
| 4 | Which Way? | Medium | LED and buzzer polarity (long leg to +) |
| 5 | Resistor Bodyguard | Hard | Match the resistor to the battery voltage |
| 6 | Circuit Builder | Hard | Build complete circuits from a box of parts |
| 7 | Switch Master | Expert | Series and parallel circuits with switches |
| 8 | Circuit Doctor | Expert | Diagnose what is wrong in broken circuits |

Open a mission directly with `#m1` … `#m8` at the end of the address, e.g. `circuit-quest/index.html#m4`.
Works with mouse or touch (tablets). Progress (stars) is saved in the browser.
